Reverend Insanity · Breeze voice cloned from Heath Miller

Every passage below was read three ways from the same text. The current voice uses one clip of calm narration for everything. The matched takes use a clip from He Who Fights with Monsters of the narrator voice that best fits the line, and each character keeps one voice for the whole chapter.

    Narrator voices

    The voices found in about four hours of He Who Fights with Monsters, grouped by how they sound and named by bonsai. Each line in the chapter is read with one of these clips.

    Names

    Each name said in a short sentence, as written and respelled. The respelling is chosen per syllable: every candidate spelling was spoken and transcribed, and the spellings heard as intended were kept. “Matched + names” uses it, along with fixes for English words the voice misread.
