**1. Reproduce: `mix test.e2e` (or `nix build .#checks.x86_64-linux.e2e -L`), look for `Reader Settings → Auto Next Chapter Toggle → ✖ toggle is visible in settings popover` with `TimeoutError: Waiting for selector #reader-settings:not(.hidden) failed`.
